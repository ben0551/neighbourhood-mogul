    How to play

    1. Each week you collect money (salary + rent) and pay bills (loans, power, rates).
    2. Watch Money Left Over. Green means healthy. Red means danger.
    3. You get 2 actions each week: study, apply for jobs, buy or sell property, renovate, pay debts.
    4. Money Makers (rental houses, businesses) put money in your pocket every week.
    5. Reach your goal: Financial Freedom means your Money Makers cover all your bills.

    Finance mode (top right) swaps the kid words for the real money words.
